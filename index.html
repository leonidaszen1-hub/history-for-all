King of Sparta, famous for his heroic stand at Thermopylae.

One of history's greatest conquerors and military commanders.

👑 Cyrus the Great

Founder of the Persian Empire and a famous ancient ruler.

A powerful Persian king who organized and expanded the empire.

Persian king known for his invasion of Greece.

One of the most famous pharaohs of Ancient Egypt.

The famous young pharaoh whose tomb became a worldwide discovery.

Babylonian king famous for his law code.

A powerful king who made Babylon one of the great cities of the ancient world.

👑 King Solomon

King of ancient Israel, traditionally remembered for wisdom.

English king famous for his military leadership during the Crusades.

⚔️ William the Conqueror

Duke of Normandy who became King of England in 1066.

👑 Charlemagne

Frankish ruler who built a large empire in Western and Central Europe.

🐎 Genghis Khan

Founder of the Mongol Empire and one of history's most influential conquerors.

👑 Akbar the Great

Mughal emperor known for administration, expansion and cultural development.

💰 Mansa Musa

Mali ruler famous for his wealth and pilgrimage to Mecca.
